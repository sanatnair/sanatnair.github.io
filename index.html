Autonomous Waste-Collection Device

Team Lead — Systems Design & Rapid Prototyping

Product Design Team Leadership Systems Integration

What

Leading the design and validation of an autonomous waste-collection robot for the ASME Student Design Competition, integrating a 4-wheel mecanum drive and 4-DOF robotic arm to optimize sorting accuracy, maneuverability, and volumetric throughput.

How

Directed a 4-member team through customer-needs translation, morphological matrix concept generation, and Pugh chart selection to finalize system architecture. Developed full-system CAD assemblies, performed center-of-mass and tipping stability analysis (≥62° forward, ≥39° lateral), and designed a full-factorial DOE to optimize dumping accuracy and cycle time across drive, collection, sorting, and delivery subsystems.

Results

Validated a final concept achieving 945 cm³/run throughput (target ≥600) and 630 cm³ storage capacity (target ≥300) while meeting all dimensional and stability constraints. Completed Critical Design Review ahead of schedule; currently transitioning to prototyping and subsystem testing for Spring 2026 competition.

RC Car Project — Capstone Mechanical Design

Project Manager — Mechanical Design & Additive Manufacturing

SolidWorks CAD Design Team Collaboration 3D Printing

What

Designed and built an RC race car with a 259 mm wheelbase, rear-wheel-drive (RWD) powertrain, and optimized Ackermann steering geometry.

How

Modeled chassis, drivetrain, and suspension in SolidWorks; fabricated components using laser-cut acrylic and 3D-printed parts. Integrated servo steering, DC motor propulsion (3.97:1 gear ratio), and U-joint driveshafts. Managed build timeline, iterative testing, and documentation for a 5-member team.

Results

Achieved 20–26 mph top speed with ~2 s 0–10 mph acceleration; completed 8 successful track laps with ~7 s best lap time. Demonstrated durability and stable handling throughout race tests.

Texas Guadaloop — Hyperloop Pod Frame & Thermal Systems

Structures & Manufacturing Team — Mechanical Design & Simulation

Product Design CAD/FEA Thermal Systems Composites

What

Designed structural and thermal subsystems for the Texas Guadaloop Hyperloop pod, including the composite chassis and propulsion cooling architecture.

How

Created a manufacturable pod frame in SolidWorks; conducted static/dynamic FEA and vibration analysis to meet strength-to-weight targets under acceleration loads. Modeled a phase-change-material-based cooling system and performed thermal simulations to ensure motor reliability. Supported fabrication via welding and composite layups.

Results

Delivered a lightweight, robust chassis meeting structural safety factors (> 2); integrated propulsion, electronics, and levitation subsystems onto the completed frame.

Human–Robot Collaboration Research — UT Nuclear & Applied Robotics Group (NRG)

Undergraduate Researcher — Robotics Systems, Perception & Hardware Integration

Robotics Mechanical Design Sensors Machine Learning AR/VR Edge Deployment ROS2

What

Building full-stack human–robot-interaction systems that blend sensing hardware, perception models, and edge ML to enable real-time and augmented reality-based (AR) human–robot collaboration for industrial and defense applications.

How

Published egocentric and allocentric perception datasets (12+ hrs, 160,000+ annotations) on the Texas Data Repository and arXiv. Fine-tuned depth- and gesture-recognition models using the UniK3D + YOLO framework and deployed inference on Jetson devices via Docker, integrating perception outputs with Microsoft HoloLens for mixed-reality human-robot interaction. Designed and fabricated a Spot-mounted Ouster LiDAR tower and a Jackal UGV payload rack to support multi-sensor field experiments. Integrated perception capabilities into a UR3 robot arm using ROS2 for additive-manufacturing tasks. Currently developing multimodal Vision-Language-Action models with agentic reinforcement learning for robust human-robot interaction under adversarial and out-of-distribution conditions.

Results

Advanced UT’s human–robot-interaction research pipeline by deploying edge-inference prototypes, developing cross-platform robotics integration, and enabling hardware testing across multiple autonomous platforms; contributed to an IEEE FG 2026 conference submission.
